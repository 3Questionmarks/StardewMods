Florist Furniture and Winter Blooms - 1.0.2

Adds 30 new flower-themed decorative items to the furniture catalog. Keeps flowers in the seasonal decorations year-round. 

:: REQUIREMENTS ::
* Content Patcher

:: FEATURES ::
* Adds more flowering houseplants to the furniture catalog
* Optional: Replaces the back-sides of end-tables with matching shop counters (obtained through the furniture catalog)
* Optional: Seasonal flowers and decorations bloom through winter (the decorations purchased at the night market)
* All pixel-art is based off of vanilla game sprites

::  KNOWN ISSUES ::
* May not be compatible with other mods that patch the furniture catalog. Contact FarmerBeans if you find any compatibility issues.
